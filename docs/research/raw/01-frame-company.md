## Public calendars and registers relevant to Frame
URLs below are from general knowledge; I did not fetch them this session, so check before citing.
- Revenue (Ireland) pay and file dates: https://www.revenue.ie/en/important-dates/ (Income Tax Form 11, CT1 returns, VAT, preliminary tax; the usual headline date is mid-October, extended via ROS for online filers). Also ROS: https://www.ros.ie
- CRO (Companies Registration Office) annual return (B1) deadlines and register search: https://www.cro.ie
- Companies Act audit exemption and financial statement filing rules: via CRO / ODCE.
- Cayman: Cayman Islands Monetary Authority (CIMA) https://www.cima.ky ; Tax Information Authority (CRS and FATCA reporting) https://www.tia.gov.ky ; Cayman Registrar of Companies annual return and Economic Substance filings via the Department for International Tax Cooperation https://www.ditc.ky ; Cayman Beneficial Ownership regime. Cayman has no income tax, so deadlines are filing-led, not tax-led.
- US: IRS due dates https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/tax-calendar ; typical dates 15 Mar (partnership/S corp), 15 Apr (individuals), 15 Oct extended deadlines; FBAR 15 Apr (auto-extended to 15 Oct); Form 5471/8938 with returns.
- Seasonality idea: Irish personal tax and CT deadlines cluster in Oct/Nov; US season is Mar-Apr then Oct; January means payroll year-end (P35 end-Feb) and accounts-year-end work; June sees CT balance and prelim tax for 30 Sep year-ends. Consistent with the slide's "Jan vs June means different things".
