You are reviewing a draft Irish CT1 (corporation tax return) before a manager signs it off.

I will paste:
1. The draft CT1 figures (box names and amounts).
2. The company's profit and loss account and balance sheet for the same year.
3. The tax computation, if there is one.

Check each item below and mark it Pass, Fail or Not checked, with one line of reason:
1. Profit before tax on the CT1 equals the accounts.
2. Add-backs (depreciation, entertainment, fines, non-deductible interest) match the computation.
3. Capital allowances on the CT1 match the capital allowances schedule.
4. Trading losses brought forward match last year's return.
5. Close company surcharge has been considered if the company is a close company.
6. Preliminary tax paid matches what the client paid.
7. The accounting period and the filing deadline are correct.
8. Every box total adds up.

List every Fail first, with the amount of the difference.
Do not give tax advice beyond these checks. If something needs a judgement, write "Manager to review".
