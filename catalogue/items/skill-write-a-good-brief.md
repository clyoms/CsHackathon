Use this before you ask AI to do a piece of client work.

Fill in the five lines below, then paste them above your request:

1. Client: [name, and the job, for example "Murphy Engineering, CT1 for year ended Feb 2026"]
2. Task: [what you want done, in one sentence, starting with a verb]
3. What I am giving you: [the files or text you will paste]
4. What I want back: [a table, a draft email, a checklist, a short summary]
5. How I will check it: [for example "I will tick three figures against the trial balance"]

Rules for the AI:
- If anything in my brief is unclear, ask me up to three short questions before you start.
- Use only the information I give you. Mark anything you are unsure of with [CHECK].
- Do not include PPS numbers, bank details or passwords in your answer.

Why this works: a vague request makes AI guess, and then you redo the work. Five lines up front usually saves the second attempt.
