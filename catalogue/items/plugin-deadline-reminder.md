A plugin adds a feature to the tool you already use.

What it does:
1. Reads your engagements and their deadlines (Revenue, IRS and Cayman dates, each with its source link).
2. Every Monday morning, shows one card per deadline in the next 30 days: client, deadline, working days left (Irish public holidays removed), and hours still budgeted.
3. On each card, suggests one task to hand to AI this week, with the matching skill from this catalogue.
4. At 10, 5 and 2 working days before a deadline, sends a short reminder: "18 Nov: Kelly Family Form 11. 5 working days left. Client documents still missing: 2."
5. Never sends anything to a client. Reminders go to you only.

Settings:
- look-ahead: 30 days
- reminder days: 10, 5, 2 working days before
- holidays: Irish public holidays for Cork and Dublin staff
- show source link: always
